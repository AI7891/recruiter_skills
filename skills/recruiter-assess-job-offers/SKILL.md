# Offer Assessment and Negotiation

## Operating contract
Use this skill independently or hand its structured result to the next relevant recruitment skill. Reuse existing candidate records; ask only for missing information material to this task. Do not require every skill in this set to run for a narrow request.
Keep candidate-specific data out of the skill itself. Use an authorized private record with source references and an updated-at timestamp. Treat missing information as unknown, not false. Distinguish facts, inferences and recommendations.
Discover available tools before relying on integrations. Read and use applicable document, calendar, messaging or storage guidance when that operation is needed. Never claim access, scheduling, sending or submission that tools have not confirmed. A skill provides instructions, not credentials, a background worker or platform permissions. If a tool, site or permission refuses an action, report it as blocked, say what the owner can do next, and do not route around the refusal.

## Inputs
Written offers, candidate preferences and relevant country/currency.

## Workflow
1. Normalize salary period, currency, gross/net basis, hours, benefits and variable-pay conditions without treating uncertain bonuses as guaranteed.
2. Compare duties, location, schedule, development, probation and other material terms using the actual written offer.
3. Verify current authoritative sources for legal, tax or regulated claims; flag issues needing qualified advice.
4. Present tradeoffs against candidate priorities and prepare specific negotiation asks supported by evidence.
5. For a freelance or contractor day rate, treat a quoted rate as gross invoiced turnover (excluding VAT unless the source says otherwise), never as take-home pay. Show the billable-days assumption, present benchmark ranges as indicative with their source and stated method, and say plainly when a source gives no method. A net figure needs current authoritative rules for the person's status; otherwise say it is not estimated. Do not recommend a single rate from general knowledge: give the sourced range, where the candidate sits in it and what evidence would move it.
6. Never accept, reject, sign or negotiate externally without explicit scope-specific authorization.

## Deliverable
Offer comparison with assumptions and unknowns; clarification questions; negotiation draft.

## Completion check
No unverified take-home estimates or unsupported market salary claims; gross and net are never confused; no automatic acceptance.
